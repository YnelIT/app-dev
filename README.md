# My Favorite Series: The Conjuring

**Genre:** Supernatural Horror  
**Director:** James Wan  
**Release Year:** 2013  

## About the Series
*The Conjuring* is a supernatural horror film series based on the real-life cases of paranormal investigators **Ed and Lorraine Warren**. The story follows them as they help families haunted by dark, demonic forces. Each movie uncovers a different case, all connected through the Warrens’ work.


## Why I Like It
I like *The Conjuring* series because of its **creepy atmosphere**, **realistic storytelling**, and **well-developed characters**. The mix of mystery and fear keeps me hooked from start to finish.

## My Favorite Scene
> When Lorraine connects spiritually with the spirit haunting the Perron family — it’s intense and emotional!

**Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ (4.5/5)
