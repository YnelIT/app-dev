# 🕰️ My Favorite Series: *Forever (2014)*

## Overview
**Forever** the main character lives forever. He can feel pain and experience death but he will just ressurect under the ocean

---

## 👥 Main Characters
- **Dr. Henry Morgan** – A 200-year-old medical examiner with a secret: he cannot die.
- **Detective Jo Martinez** – Henry’s NYPD partner.
- **Lucas Wahl** – Henry’s assistant in the morgue.
